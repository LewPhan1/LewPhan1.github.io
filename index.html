About

Who am I?

I’m a research scientist with a particular interest in pharmacometrics and quantitative drug development through mechanistic modelling. My work has taken me from agent-based models and experimental bioscience during my PhD to ODE-based modelling and membrane reconstitution in my current postdoctoral research at the University of Sheffield. An internship at Certara Simcyp sparked my interest in PB/PK and pharmacometrics, which I’ve continued to develop through certified training, independent modelling projects, and meetings such as PAGE and ISSX. This blog is a record of that journey as I build my skills in PBPK/DMPK, AI, and model-informed drug development.

Personal projects that interest me :)

Midazolam PB/PK DDI

Modelling midazolam pharmacokinetics and ketoconazole-mediated CYP3A4 inhibition using a custom-built Perfusion-limited PBPK model. Calibrating IV disposition, translating to oral dosing, validating against clinical PK and DDI datasets, and applying sensitivity analysis to explore the drivers of AUC, Cmax, half-life, and AUCR.

Python ODEs (solve_ivp) PB/PK PK fitting

Agentic PBPK Workflow

A seven-agent AI PBPK evidence workflow that searches literature and drug labels for model parameters, extracts, resolves conflicting sources, converts units, and flags uncertain or corrupted data. Uses Europe PMC, openFDA, local papers, and deterministic Python validation, with human approval before running a mechanistic midazolam–ketoconazole PBPK model.

In my current postdoctoral research, I use mechanistic ODE models to understand membrane lipid-identity switching and interpret quantitative experimental data. I also developed an in vitro phosphatase system using planar lipid bilayers and microscopy, generating controlled datasets to parameterise, test, and refine these models.

PhD in mechanistic systems biology

University of Sheffield · October 2018 – December 2023

During my PhD in computational systems biology, I combined experimental biochemistry with mechanistic modelling to study the spatial organisation and competitive regulation of endocytosis. I developed a de novo agent-based model in C/C++, parallelised it on a GPU, and used it to generate hypotheses and guide in vitro experiments. A key part of the project was iterating between modelling and experiment, using each to inform and refine the other.

Internship in pharmacometrics

Certara Simcyp · October 2020 – January 2021

My internship at Certara Simcyp was where my interest in pharmacokinetics and PBPK really began to take shape. I worked on Python packages and API workflows for preclinical toxicokinetic and PK data, analysed exposure and concentration–toxicity relationships, and gained first-hand insight into how mechanistic PK models are developed and applied through discussions with the Simcyp team.
